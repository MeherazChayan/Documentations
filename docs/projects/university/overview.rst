System concepts and interaction boundaries
==========================================

My University Portal is a Next.js 16 and React 19 application written in
TypeScript. Its interface brings together advising, academic schedules,
scholarship and waiver information, attendance and university service views.

The assistant presents contextual answers and navigation suggestions. The
public repository describes these responses as **deterministic client-side
rules**, not calls to a connected external AI model.

Delegation settings illustrate different levels of authority: what an
assistant may recommend, prepare, request confirmation for or refrain from
executing. They support exploration of how human approval and boundaries can
be communicated in a student-facing interface.

Simulation boundary
-------------------

All displayed student identities, academic records, awards, dates,
recommendations and actions are fictional or simulated. The prototype is not
connected to university information systems, a payment provider or an external
AI model. Service buttons illustrate possible workflows, not real enrollment,
financial or administrative operations.

Source: `My University Portal README <https://github.com/MeherazChayan/My-University-Portal#readme>`_.
